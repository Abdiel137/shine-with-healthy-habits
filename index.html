Who we are

Small habits. Lasting possibilities.

SHINE With Healthy Habits Project is a community-based child development and education initiative in Musanze, Rwanda. We bring children together for learning, positive routines and activities that support their mental and physical well-being.

🎯

Our Mission

To create a supportive community environment where children develop regular reading habits and healthy mental and physical abilities.

🌱

Our Vision

A community where every child can develop healthy habits and build a brighter future.

🤝

Our Values

Learning, health, responsibility and inclusion guide the way we work with children and families.

What we do

Activities that help children thrive.

We make learning active, welcoming and connected to everyday life.

📖

Reading & Learning

Community reading, lesson revision and opportunities to build confidence with books and schoolwork.

🧩

Mind & Creativity

Puzzles, quizzes, riddles, memory games, storytelling, poetry and cultural activities.

🏃

Movement & Play

Weekend sports and physical activities that encourage teamwork and active routines.

🌞

Healthy Daily Habits

School and community learning about simple habits that support well-being and responsibility.

Our approach

A safe place to learn together.

Children benefit when families, schools and communities work together. SHINE aims to provide welcoming, inclusive and age-appropriate activities with suitable supervision.

  • Encouraging curiosity and regular reading
  • Making room for movement, games and creativity
  • Promoting inclusion, responsibility and healthy routines
  • Working with families and the wider community
